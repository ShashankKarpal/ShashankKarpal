01

Five rules, plus what to do when in doubt

1. Colour tells you the group, the mark tells you the project. Three colours cover everything. You will never have to remember which of nine colours means which app.
2. Category on the left, status on the right. Category colour appears only on a left rail, a mark or a badge. Status appears only in a chip, and every chip carries a word.
3. Ink is a category too. Making, 3D printing, forks and the profile repo carry no accent. Absence of colour is information.
4. Grain is the only texture. Banners, covers and empty states. Never behind text, tables or charts.
5. Twelve pixels between rows, minimum. No italics, no underlines, no emoji, no em dashes. Emphasis comes from weight and size.
When in doubt, use ink and one accent. A screen with two colours on it is always readable; a screen with six is a legend you have to decode.

07

Grain, the only texture

Dark theme, opacity 0.16. Visible as texture, invisible as pattern.

Allowed

README header bands, app empty states, hero and cover panels, PDF and deck covers.

Not allowed

Behind body text. Inside tables. Behind charts or sparklines. Under any type below 14px. On top of a category or status fill.

feTurbulence fractalNoise, baseFrequency 0.9, numOctaves 3, saturate 0, opacity 0.16 dark and 0.07 light.

The grid, dot field and circle patterns you also liked are held in reserve, not in the system. If grain ever proves too quiet for a specific surface, the dot field at 0.5px on edge colour is the next step up, and it needs a deliberate decision, not a habit.
08

Logo and wordmark rules

Clear space and size

zest

Clear space is half the x-height of the wordmark on all four sides, measured from the ink, not the box. The dashed line above marks the ink; nothing enters the padding around it.

Minimum size: 96px wide on screen, 28mm in print. Project marks: 16px absolute minimum, and they must be drawn at 512, 24 and 16 on both light and dark ground before use.

Colour and backgrounds

The wordmark is always text colour, never a category colour. At most one letter may carry a filled dot in the project's category colour, exactly as your banner already does.

Approved backgrounds

#0B0C0D, #171614, #F5F5F3, #FFFFFF

Never

Recolour it to an accent. Stretch, skew, rotate or outline it. Add shadow, glow or bevel. Place it on a mid grey between #404040 and #A0A0A0. Place it over grain above 0.2, or over a photograph. Commit a banner block to a repo before the artwork exists, in public or in private.

Four collisions, stated rather than hidden

Brass against Neutral measures 6.1, Brass against Good 12.0, Mist against Info 13.5, and Copper against Problem 19.8 under red green colour blindness. Eight meaningful colours in a limited brightness range cannot all separate; that is arithmetic, not a design failure.

They are safe because of rule 2. A category colour only ever appears on a left rail, a mark or a badge. A status colour only ever appears in a chip carrying a word. The two never share a slot, so the eye never has to tell them apart.

Beyond colour

Twelve pixel minimum between rows. No type below 11px, and 11px only for tracked labels. Grain banned behind text, tables and charts. No italics or underlines, so emphasis is weight or size. Focus is a 2px Mist ring at 14.33 on dark and 2px #2D647F on light, always with 2px offset. Re-run verify-palette.py after any colour change; it exits non-zero if a required pair fails.

11

Open items, carried honestly

The four marks exist as banner PNGs, not as a mark set. ledge, helios, zest and switchdeck already carry live 1400x400 README banners, so those need re-rendering in this palette. What does not exist is the four locked shapes as reusable production SVGs at 512, 24 and 16 on both grounds. Draw those before any new repo gets a banner block.
Mark to project mapping is unrecorded. Nothing in your history says which shape belongs to which app. That is a decision waiting for you, not something to infer.
Capitals. Karpal Geometric is lowercase only. Capital keys produce the lowercase shapes so nothing breaks. Real capitals are a separate project.
helios tokens.json. Its badge hex and its token file drifted apart once. Reconcile both to Brass in the same commit.
The profile clone is behind the remote. Pull with fast forward before editing the banner locally, or you will recreate the drift.
Making and 3D printing. Ink for now, by your decision. Revisit only when it has something worth colouring.
