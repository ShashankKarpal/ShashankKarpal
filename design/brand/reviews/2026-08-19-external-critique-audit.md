# External critique audit, public record

Status: historical, sanitized, and superseded by the 2026-08-20 remediation.

The original audit compared an external Ink and Bone critique with the live
repositories. It was unusually accurate and directly caused a second round of
privacy, accessibility, build, asset, and operations work.

The full audit is retained in the private operations repository. It is not
published here because it aggregated private project names, local
infrastructure, and operational topology. This public record deliberately
contains no private identifier geometry, private project inventory, machine
address, service endpoint, daemon name, or local filesystem topology.

## Durable findings

- A private identifier had already been disclosed in public history. Its v1
  form was retired, current public trees were sanitized, and history rewriting
  was rejected as disproportionate after disclosure.
- The public and private operating procedures needed a real trust boundary,
  not comments and ignore rules alone.
- The claimed reproducible build, accessibility verifier, consumer provenance,
  and generated-asset parity each required stronger executable checks.
- The avatar and several small marks needed art-directed small-size outputs,
  rather than one composition scaled to every size.
- Palette, type, grain, and wordmark rules needed to be consumed from the
  canonical token source and tested against actual outputs.

## Record policy

This file is a public incident summary, not the implementation log or the
current normative specification. Current public rules live in
`brand-tokens.json`, `BRAND-SURFACES.md`, and `../README.md`. Detailed private
operations and the original evidence remain in the private repository.
