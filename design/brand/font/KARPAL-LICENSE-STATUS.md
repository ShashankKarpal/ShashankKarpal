# Karpal Geometric license status

Status: owner decision required before third-party reuse.

The embedded font metadata identifies Shashank Karpal as the owner and
describes the face as custom work developed with Claude. No separate license
grant was found in the repositories or reachable history during the
2026-08-20 audit. The font is therefore not presented as SIL OFL, MIT, or
otherwise open source.

It may continue to be used in Shashank Karpal's own repositories, products,
and brand materials. Do not redistribute it as a standalone font, modify it
for an unrelated identity, or claim an open-font license until the owner has
chosen and recorded explicit terms. This status note is operational guidance,
not a substitute for that license decision.
